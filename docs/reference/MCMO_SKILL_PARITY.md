## Child and secondary relationships

The baseline has 19 primary skills and 81 subskills. Salvage and Smelting are child skills in the fixed reference; their progress is derived from their parent skills and excluded from power level. BigBangSkills keeps the canonical IDs and derives Salvage from Repair/Fishing and Smelting from Mining/Repair.

Subskill groups in the baseline: Acrobatics (Dodge, Roll); Alchemy (Catalysis, Concoctions); Archery (Arrow Retrieval, Daze, Skill Shot, Archery Limit Break); Axes (Armor Impact, Axe Mastery, Axes Limit Break, Critical Strikes, Greater Impact, Skull Splitter); Crossbows (Crossbows Limit Break, Trick Shot, Powered Shot); Excavation (Archaeology, Giga Drill Breaker); Fishing (Fisherman's Diet, Ice Fishing, Magic Hunter, Master Angler, Treasure Hunter, Shake); Herbalism (Double Drops, Verdant Bounty, Farmer's Diet, Green Terra, Green Thumb, Hylian Luck, Shroom Thumb); Maces (Maces Limit Break, Crush, Cripple); Mining (Bigger Bombs, Blast Mining, Demolitions Expertise, Double Drops, Super Breaker, Mother Lode); Repair (Arcane Forging, Repair Mastery, Super Repair); Salvage (Scrap Collector, Arcane Salvage); Smelting (Fuel Efficiency, Second Smelt, Understanding the Art); Spears (Spears Limit Break, Momentum, Spear Mastery); Swords (Counter Attack, Rupture, Serrated Strikes, Stab, Swords Limit Break); Taming (Beast Lore, Call of the Wild, Environmentally Aware, Fast Food Service, Gore, Holy Hound, Pummel, Sharpened Claws, Shock Proof, Thick Fur); Tridents (Impale, Tridents Limit Break); Unarmed (Arrow Deflect, Berserk, Block Cracker, Disarm, Steel Arm Style, Iron Grip, Unarmed Limit Break); Woodcutting (Knock on Wood, Harvest Lumber, Leaf Blower, Tree Feller, Clean Cuts).
